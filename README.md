Outono Adventures is a 3D adventure game where a german girl named Vanessa discovers that her best friend, Amanda, whom she hasn't seen in weeks, is trapped inside a video game.

However, instead of breaking the CD to save her friend, Vanessa decides to try playing the game and ends up being dragged inside. She is then transformed into a court jester named Outono, and she will face many adventures, far beyond just jumping on enemies' heads, to find her friend and return to the real world.

Features:

- 8 Levels Doors and 1 Boss Level.

- Volumes Slider from Music & SFX

- Full Screen or Window mode toggle.

- FPS Counter.

- Resolution settings.

- Quality settings (plays on low, medium, high or ultra graphics).

Controls (they can be remapped to your liking in-game settings; this is the first game of mine that allows to do that.):

-WASD move the Player (but in certain phases it's also used to move the Kart and the Car driving);

-Space for Jump and Double Jump (but in certain phases her can brake the kart, brake the car and shoot in zombies).

-E for Interaction (for example, for enter the circus, enter the levels, and many other things...)

-ESC for Pause the Game.

-F12 for Capture a Screenshot (It is saved by default in the My Pictures folder.)

-Z for Kick a Ball (only when she touches a ball, but in the kart phase, she can generate and throw balls at enemies.)

-Mouse controls the camera.
